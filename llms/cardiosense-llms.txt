# Cardiosense

> Cardiosense is a medical technology company based in Chicago, IL, developing AI-powered cardiac monitoring solutions. Our flagship product, the CardioTag™, is the world's first noninvasive Heart Failure monitoring device that delivers Pulmonary Capillary Wedge Pressure (PCWP) measurements at the point of care.

## What We Do

Cardiosense enables clinicians to more precisely and proactively manage Heart Failure patients by delivering hemodynamic data noninvasively — without the risks, costs, or complications of invasive monitoring.

The CardioTag™ device and PCWP Analysis Software are FDA-cleared as a Class II medical device. The software is intended for prescription use under the supervision of a qualified healthcare professional, in conjunction with clinical history, symptoms, and other diagnostic tests.

## Our Technology

- **Wearable biosensor** — The CardioTag™ attaches to the patient’s chest and captures cardiac hemodynamic data at the point of care
- **Noninvasive PCWP** — Pulmonary Capillary Wedge Pressure measured without invasive catheterization or implantable sensors
- **AI signal processing** — Deep learning converts raw physiological data into clinical-grade intelligence
- **Clinician portal** — Real-time trending and longitudinal tracking of PCWP data for clinical decision support
- **Validated accuracy** — Accuracy on par with implantable sensors; validated against right heart catheterization (gold standard)

## Clinical Applications

- **ED Assessment & Triage** — Rapidly confirm Heart Failure at point-of-care to initiate medical therapy sooner and reduce hospitalization length-of-stay
- **Inpatient Management & Discharge** — Drive informed clinical changes with routine PCWP assessment; discharge patients with confidence
- **Transitions & Remote Monitoring** — Monitor PCWP remotely during the highest-risk post-discharge window; detect worsening HF early; optimize GDMT
